Privacy Policy

Publisher: SparkyMate · Updated 7 November 2026

Contact: electricalservicegreens@gmail.com

SparkyMate is an electrical toolbox for New Zealand and Australia. The app is being prepared for release. Paid Pro purchasing and its verification server are not yet live. This policy must be updated with the verification hosting provider and retention settings before those services are enabled.

Your work records stay on your device

Names, company and licence details, customer contact details, job addresses, test readings, notes and reports entered into SparkyMate are saved locally. SparkyMate does not upload these work records to a cloud account. Android uses app-private storage with automatic Android backup disabled. Browser data belongs to the browser profile and the exact app website address. There is no SparkyMate user account or sign-in.

Optional online address search

When you press Search, your search text and selected country are sent over HTTPS to Photon at photon.komoot.io, provided by Komoot, to retrieve OpenStreetMap address suggestions. The provider also receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Address searches are optional: you can enter a job address manually.

The Photon project's published maintainer guidance states that request logs are retained for a limited period to operate the service. We do not control those logs and do not promise immediate deletion or a specific retention period. Search text is therefore not treated as memory-only processing. Avoid including names or unrelated confidential information in searches. See Photon's request-log explanation and Komoot's privacy policy.

Planned Pro purchases

The planned optional one-time Pro upgrade uses Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; SparkyMate does not receive card or bank details. Purchase verification and restoration are designed to send a purchase token and product identifier over HTTPS to a SparkyMate verification service, which checks the purchase with Google Play. Customer work records are not included.

The verification implementation does not intentionally save purchase tokens in an application database or application logs. Google and the future hosting provider may retain transaction or operational records. Verification hosting and its retention settings have not yet been selected or deployed. Purchases must remain disabled until this policy and the app's disclosures describe the actual live service. See Google's privacy policy.

PDF, backups, clipboard and email

Reports are generated locally. PDF export uses your browser or Android print service. Copying records or backups places their content on the device clipboard. Email opens a draft in a separate email application; you choose the recipient, attachments and whether to send. Any email, print, storage or sharing provider you choose handles exported information under its own policies. SparkyMate does not automatically send reports to its server.

Advertising and analytics

SparkyMate currently includes no advertising SDK or analytics SDK. It does not request GPS location, camera or contacts access for its current features.

Retention and deletion

Local work records remain until you delete them or clear the app's storage. On Android, use Settings → Apps → SparkyMate → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the app's site data. These actions do not delete exported backups, PDFs, clipboard copies or sent emails; delete those separately in the apps where you saved or sent them.

No online account-deletion process exists because there is no SparkyMate cloud account. We do not currently offer a verified deletion-request process for third-party address-service logs or Google's transaction records. Contact support for privacy questions; do not send customer records unnecessarily.

Security

Online address requests use HTTPS. The planned purchase verifier requires HTTPS. Local records and exported backups are not additionally encrypted by SparkyMate. Protect your device and backup files, and keep separate copies of important work.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when features or data practices change. For questions about SparkyMate's handling of information, email electricalservicegreens@gmail.com.
